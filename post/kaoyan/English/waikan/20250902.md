- **trials** 磨难 考验

- **athlete** 田径运动员

<br />

Born in 1997 in Zigong, in the southwestern Sichuan province, Wu was an <span class="red">energetic</span> child, initially studying dancing. At 9 years old, she took part in a sports contest in Neijiang, also in Sichuan, when she was spotted by a coach who saw her <span class="red">potential</span> as a <span class="red">track and field</span> athlete. After some time training in the provincial capital Chengdu, she was selected for the Beijing Sports University <span class="red">hurdles</span> team in 2012, marking the start of her career. She made her <span class="red">debut</span> in China's national <span class="red">athletics squad</span> in 2019.

> 吴艳妮1997年出生在四川省西南部的自贡市，她从小活泼好动，一开始学的是舞蹈。9岁那年，她在四川内江参加了一场体育比赛，当时一位教练看中了她，认为她有成为田径运动员的潜力。在四川省会成都训练了一段时间后，2012年她被选入北京体育大学跨栏队，由此开始了她的运动生涯。2019年，吴艳妮首次入选中国国家田径队。

- **energetic** 精力充沛的
- **potential** 潜力的
- **track and field** 田径运动
- **hurdles** 跨栏
- **debut** 出道 首次亮相
- **squad** 运动队

<br />

The <span class="red">mega</span>-famous couple shared a five-photo <span class="red">carousel</span> of Swift and Kelce, both 35, surrounded by flowers with Travis on one knee. ### Donald Trump to Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce: 'I wish them a lot of luck'

<br />

President Donald Trump hasn't always been Taylor Swift's biggest fan, but he had warm wishes for the musician and her football player fiancee Travis Kelce on their engagement. "I think he's a great player. I think he's a great guy," Trump said at the White House on Tuesday. "And I think that she's a <span class="red">terrific</span> person, so I wish them a lot of luck." Trump has repeatedly <span class="red">slammed</span> Swift, who <span class="red">endorsed</span> his 2024 Democratic <span class="red">opponent</span> Kamala Harris.

> 唐纳德·特朗普总统并非泰勒·斯威夫特的歌迷，但这位音乐人与其橄榄球明星未婚夫特拉维斯·凯尔西订婚时，他送上了暖心祝福。特朗普周二在白宫表示："我认为他是名出色的球员，也是个很棒的小伙子。她也是个非常优秀的人，所以我祝他们好运。"斯威夫特曾公开支持特朗普 2024 年大选的民主党竞争对手卡玛拉·哈里斯，特朗普此前多次对她大加抨击。

- **terrific** 极好的
- **slam** 抨击
- **endorse** 支持
- **opponent** 竞争对手
